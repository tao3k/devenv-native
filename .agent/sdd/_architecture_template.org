:SDD_KIND: capability

:SDD_STATUS: draft
:SDD_SLUG: capability-sdd-title

*** Requirement: Architecture calibration
The system SHALL keep implementation plans linked to accepted design decisions
and explicit architecture concerns.

**** Scenario: Agent inspects design drift
- WHEN an Agent queries SDD status
- THEN the SDD SHALL expose architecture nodes, parent edges, concerns,
  viewpoints, and rationale without implementation task progress.

** Design Decision Title :sdd:decision:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 00000000-0000-7000-8000-000000000004
:SDD_KIND: decision

*** Decision
State the chosen architecture direction and the alternatives rejected.

*** Consequences
- Consequence: describe a durable design constraint.
- Risk: describe how future plans might drift from the design.

** Architecture Audit Title :sdd:audit:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 00000000-0000-7000-8000-000000000005
:SDD_KIND: audit
:SDD_PARENT: [[id:00000000-0000-7000-8000-000000000002][Capability SDD Title]]
:SDD_STATUS: draft
:SDD_CONCERN: Evidence required before implementation can be considered aligned.
:SDD_QUALITY: correctness, performance, maintainability
:SDD_SLUG: architecture-audit-title
:END:

*** Audit Questions
- Question: Does the implementation preserve the documented ownership boundary?
- Question: Does the evidence prove the target quality attribute?

*** Fitness Criteria
- Gate: record the measurable condition for accepting an implementation plan.
- Gate: record the rollback or follow-up condition if evidence regresses.

*** Linked Implementation
Implementation work belongs in an Org task or ExecPlan that links back to this
SDD through =SDD= or =SDD_REF= properties.
